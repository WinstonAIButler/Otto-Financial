        Questions
        Before you start
        
      
        
        
        Is Otto Funded trading real money?
        Evaluation and funded accounts trade in a simulated environment that mirrors live market prices. Otto Financial pays performance rewards based on your simulated results under the trader agreement.
        
        
        What happens if I break a rule?
        Hitting the daily or overall loss limit ends that account. You can start a new evaluation at a 15% discount within 7 days.
        
        
        Can I use the Challenge Toolkit at other prop firms?
        Yes, as long as that firm allows it. The toolkit is a risk manager and planner. Several firms prohibit expert advisors, copy trading or trade-management software, so check each program's terms first. Otto Funded allows the toolkit on every account.
        
        
        Will Otto Quant make me profitable?
        No tool can promise that. Otto Quant helps you state a strategy precisely and test it honestly. Many ideas fail in backtesting, and that is a useful result that costs you nothing.
        
        
        Can Otto Agents place trades for me?
        No. Agents read your account and rules, explain what they found and suggest a next step. Anything that would open, close or resize a position needs your confirmation, and you can turn agents off at any time.
        
        
        When are payouts sent?
        You can request a payout every 14 days once funded. Requests are reviewed and paid within two business days by bank transfer or USDC.
